- **Verdict (2026-09-18):** NOT free. itHappyStudios is a commercial vendor (all of
  their CGTrader/ArtStation city packs are paid products; comparable Japan packs on
  CGTrader run ~$99–$249). No FREE badge on the page; exact price not visible via
  page-text fetch — re-confirm on the live page at approval time.
- **License:** not CC0 / not free — would be CGTrader's standard per-purchase license
  (royalty-bearing commercial use in one project; terms on the live page).
- **Formats:** Unity Package, UE, Blender, C4D, Maya, 3DSMax, **FBX, OBJ, GLTF**, STL.
  Godot 4 route: **GLTF direct import** (page lists "Godot Engine 3.4+" compatibility;
  GLTF/FBX import works in 4.x).
- **Contents:** 73 low-poly assets — 20 buildings (pagodas, temples, castles, shrines),
  13 landscape elements (torii bases, sakura parks, ponds), 6 roads/parking (crossings,
  traffic lights, road signs), 12 props (lanterns, torii gates, parasols), 22 vehicles,
  plus a demo scene.
- **Tech:** 209K triangles whole pack (~2.9K avg/asset — very light), real-world scale,
  **3 materials (color, emission, glass)** + 1×1024 universal palette PNG.
- **Fit note:** stylized traditional-Japan, NOT cyberpunk by default — needs night
  lighting/emissive treatment to sit in a neon racer. The built-in *emission* material
  is neon-friendly (lanterns/signs could glow with minimal work). Good candidate for a
  Japan-district / night-market area; would clash with a pure neon-downtown street if
  mixed directly.
